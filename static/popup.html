Quick guide
GainInput level before EQ. Percent and dB show the same setting in two forms.
EQDrag a point to change frequency and gain. Click a point for precise controls. On Peak bands, mouse wheel or Shift-drag changes Q. Shelf Q is not used by Web Audio.
DynamicsControls peaks and level changes. 3-band can treat lows, mids and highs separately.
StereoMid/Side width, balance, mono and L/R swap. Stereo sits before Protection so widening peaks are still visible to the limiter.
PitchLowers tone by up to 12 semitones without changing playback speed. Upward shifting was removed because speech quality was not reliable enough.
EffectsDown Pitch, Reverb and 8D are grouped under Effects. Reverb and 8D are post-Protection creative stages; Pitch stays before Dynamics. Delay and Exciter were retired to keep the extension focused and easier to use.
ProtectionSafety limiter after Stereo and before decorative effects. It is global and is not stored in presets.
Panel positions are only workspace layout. Dragging a panel never changes the audio signal order.
Presets
Selected preset
Presets apply the EQ curve. Gain, DSP modules, Protection and panel positions stay independent.
Clip protection
Final output safety. Strong is recommended for aggressive Gain or EQ. Uses the compressor-only protection path so the new modules do not add extra saturation.
